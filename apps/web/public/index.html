04 Benchmark

AUROC / Brier Skill vs. baselines, per outcome · horizon · forecaster. Minimum 100 resolved to show a metric, 200 (with ≥30 positives) to claim a beat. "Beats X" means the ranking is statistically distinguishable from X's (DeLong test) — not that the probability itself is well-calibrated; Brier Skill is shown alongside for that.

Scored rows are live forecasts whose commit landed on-chain (by block time) within 30 minutes of the report's T+10m anchor and before the outcome's horizon ended. Late commits, outage replays and uncommitted reports are excluded and counted under each outcome. No backfilled rows.
